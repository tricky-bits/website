# About Tricky Bits

**Tricky Bits** is a home for handcrafted puzzles — logical riddles,
architectural challenges, and the occasional dirty trick. Puzzles are grouped
into **campaigns**: short, themed series of **stages** you work through one at a
time.

## How it works

- Each stage gives you something to figure out and an answer box.
- Answers are checked **right in your browser** — nothing is sent to a server.
- Solving a stage unlocks the next one in the campaign.
- Stuck? Stages can offer **timed hints**, and some ship **input files** to work
  from.

No accounts, no sign-up, no tracking. Your progress lives in your own browser.

## New here?

Start with the **Getting Started** campaign on the [home page](/) — it teaches
the mechanics while you play, no prior knowledge required.

## Open source

Tricky Bits is built with an open-source static-site generator. The tooling and
this site's source live on
[GitHub](https://github.com/tricky-bits). If it's useful to you, consider
leaving a star.
