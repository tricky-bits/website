payments, and no subscriptions. The site is provided as-is, for entertainment and
educational purposes.

# Privacy

Tricky Bits is a **client-side** puzzle platform. We do not run a backend that
tracks you, profiles you, or stores your data on our servers.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** We do not operate accounts, analytics, ad networks, or third-party
trackers on this site.

## What stays on your device

Tricky Bits uses your browser's `localStorage` to save:

- Your progress through challenges
- Which hints you have revealed
- Your submitted answers
- Completion state of puzzle collections

This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is scoped to the browser and device you are using
- Can be exported, restored, or wiped at any time from **"Manage Progress"** in
  the footer

## Hosting

The site is served as static files. Standard web server access logs (IP, user
agent, requested path) may be retained by the hosting provider for operational
and security purposes. We do not link these logs to any identity and do not use
them for tracking.

## External links

Pages on Tricky Bits may link to external sites (GitHub, references inside
individual puzzles). Once you follow such a link, the destination's own privacy
policy applies. We have no control over how those sites handle your data.

## Cookies

Tricky Bits does not set cookies. Only `localStorage` is used, and only for the
progress data described above.

## Children

Tricky Bits collects no personal data from anyone, including children. The
platform is safe to use without an account.

## Changes

These terms and privacy practices may be updated over time. Continued use of the
site means you accept the current version. Because no accounts exist, there is no
way to notify users individually — please check back if you want to stay current.
