# Terms & Privacy

## Privacy

Tricky Bits is a **static site**. There is no backend, no account system, and no
analytics. Any progress you make solving stages is stored **locally in your own
browser** (via `localStorage`) and never leaves your device.

- Clearing your browser data clears your progress.
- Nothing you do here is uploaded anywhere.
- No tracking cookies are set.

## Sharing

You can share a direct link to any stage. Anyone with the link can open it — no
account or prior progress required.

## Fair play

Answer checking happens in your browser, so a determined visitor can find ways
around a puzzle. Please don't spoil them for everyone else — the fun is in the
solving.

## No warranty

Tricky Bits is provided as-is, for entertainment. The puzzles, hints, and any
downloadable files are offered without warranty of any kind.
