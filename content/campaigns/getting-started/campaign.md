Welcome to **Getting Started**, the gentlest campaign on Tricky Bits.

Most campaigns ask you to *figure out* the answer. This one does the opposite:
it walks you through how everything works and hands you every answer, so you can
get comfortable without a puzzle getting in the way.

By the end you'll know:

1. What a **campaign** and a **stage** are.
2. How **answering** works.
3. How **hints** work when you're stuck.
4. How **input files** reach you.

A handful of short stages. Let's go.
