**downloadable chip in the info panel below**, sitting alongside the tags and
details.

this text, download it, and open it.

Unlike the earlier stages, the answer isn't written on this page. It's **inside
the file**. Open `briefing.txt`, find the `codeword:` line, and type what it says
to continue.
