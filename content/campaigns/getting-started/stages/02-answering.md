Every stage has a body (this text), an optional set of hints, and an answer box.

To complete a stage you type the answer and submit it. A few things to know:

- Answers are checked **right in your browser** — they're never sent to a server.
- Checking is **case-insensitive**, and surrounding spaces are ignored. So
  `Browser`, `browser`, and ` browser ` all count.
- The expected answer is never shipped in plain text; only a hash of it reaches
  your browser, so peeking at the page source won't give it away.

Where is an answer checked? Right in your **browser**.
