Most stages give you a body of text to read and an answer box to fill in.

To clear a stage you type the answer and submit it. A couple of things to know:

- Checking is **case-insensitive**, so `Solve`, `solve`, and `SOLVE` all count.
- Surrounding spaces are ignored too, so ` solve ` is fine.

So what are you here to do with a stage? You **solve** it.
