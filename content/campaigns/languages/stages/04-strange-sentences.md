Some peoples wrote with letters. Some wrote with little pictures. **This** one
wrote with *equations*. Their scribes kept whole stories in ledgers that look,
at a glance, like a page of algebra — every sentence balanced around an `=`,
every idea folded into a fraction or tucked beneath a sign.

You've recovered eight lines of their writing, and for each one a kind soul
left the meaning beside it. Study how the symbols move — what marks a plural,
what flips a sentence into the negative, who is *doing* and who is *done to* —
then use what you learn to read the final line entirely on your own.
