The first page of the notebook isn't meant to be *seen* at all. Run a finger
across it and you feel them: tiny raised bumps, arranged in neat little cells.
This is a writing system you read by *touch*, not by sight.

The collector was kind enough to translate the first three lines. Study how the
patterns of dots line up with the words, then use what you learn to read the
fourth line on your own.
