Not every language is written with letters. **Semanto** is a *semantic*
writing system: every little drawing stands for a whole concept, the way a heart
on a card means "love" no matter what language you speak.

Below are ten Semanto-words, labelled **A** through **J**. Each one matches exactly
one word in the list underneath. Your job is to **work out the correct
correspondence between every image and its meaning.**
