The **Korvani** people count in a way that looks strange at first — but every
numeral is built from a handful of words and two tiny connectors. Crack the
pattern and you can read any number they write.

Here are some numerals in Korvani and their values:

| Numeral | Value |
|:--|--:|
| wejo | 2 |
| binamemo rote | 16 |
| woraiwamo jako rote | 38 |
| eji mipejo | 39 |
| edebemo rote bia | 55 |
| mipeno doimeto maita | 80 |
| inomo rote bia doimeto maita | 111 |
| maita wejo mo eji ko weda | 129 |
| maita wejo mo eji jako rote ko weda | 149 |
| maita edebe mo wedamo mipejo ko biname | 343 |

**Your task — write each of these in digits:**

1. `maita woraiwa`
2. `ejimo jako rote`
3. `maita weda mo biname ko we`


Give your three numbers **in order**, joined with hyphens.

Example format: `123-45-678`
