Barry has a habit of using code everywhere.

Not just at work, in his notes, in his reminders, sometimes even in places where it makes absolutely no sense.

On his desk, you find a scrap of paper. At the top, written in a different hand, it simply says:

> Caesar's salad

Below it is a short sequence of characters:

> Bcpcrm fsasapsfg kvm hvwg gmghsa bssrg o acbhvzm fsgsh. W'a bch hvs cbs pfsoywbu hvoh hforwhwcb.

Barry left no explanation. He rarely did.

Whatever this note was meant to represent, it clearly mattered enough to be part of the reset.

Decode the sequence and submit the value you find.
