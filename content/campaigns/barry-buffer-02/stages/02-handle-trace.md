The suspect was careful with his data and careless with himself. He reused one handle
everywhere, the way over-documenters do: a single label, indexed forever.

Barry circled it once on a printout and wrote nothing else. Clipped behind it, three screenshots
he pulled before he went dark. They trace the handle to the one thing the suspect could not
bring himself to encrypt: his archive of a pet he loved more than operational security.
