The case file the Bureau handed you is thin. Most of it is Barry's, which means most of it is
unreadable on purpose.

Clipped to the inside cover is a sticky note. Barry's handwriting, one line above a short token:

> rotate the alphabet, nothing fancy

```
pbyqobbg
```

Beneath it, a second line, underlined twice:

> This is the internal name for the case. Submit it and we begin.

No shift is given. Barry assumes you know the default.

Decode the token and submit the case name.
