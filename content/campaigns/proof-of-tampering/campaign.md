**Proof of Tampering** is a math-forensics campaign. You play a forensic analyst
hired to reconstruct what really happened when a research notebook vanished from
the Meridian Institute.

Each stage is self-contained, but the campaign has a narrative thread: every
solved challenge reveals one more piece of the case board. The final stage uses
evidence recovered from earlier stages.

You'll work through modular arithmetic, timeline reconstruction, steganography,
forensic accounting, graph ordering, RSA, affine ciphers, and a final
Vigenère-style reconstruction. Flags are lowercase words separated by
underscores.

Eight stages. The evidence doesn't lie, but someone tried to make it.
